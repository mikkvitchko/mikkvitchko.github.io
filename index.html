Hi, I’m Mik.

I’m a software engineer, designer, and longtime builder of things that involve computers. Back in 1991, Michael Markov and I co-founded Markov Processes International. The next year we made an early commercial tool for returns-based investment style analysis. My side of the adventure has mostly been software, system design, and user interfaces—because even very serious numbers deserve a decent place to live.

Before MPI, I worked in software engineering and at Coopers & Lybrand. I studied mechanical engineering at Kharkov Polytechnic University. These days, my off-screen interests include AI assisted development and wingfoiling: two activities that both reward patience, though only one comes with a wetsuit.

That’s the short version. For the more official one, see my MPI profile.
